───────────────────────────────

         │

────────────────────────────
16:00 → Dashboard (P4) → Ajukan Cuti (P4.1) → Pending
         │
         └─ D4:cuti ◄─► Database
         
    ↓ NEXT DAY ↓
    
HRD Review (P4.3) → Approve/Reject (P4.4) → Disetujui/Ditolak
 │
 └─ D4:cuti ◄─► Database
